# Five-Power Scoring Rubric

## Global anchors

- **10 — Exceptional:** award-caliber work; strategy, concept, structure, and language are nearly flawless and ready for a major global client.
- **7–8 — Professional:** meets agency review standards and serves the business objective effectively; only focused refinement is needed.
- **5–6 — Competent draft:** the core idea works, but senior revision is still needed in strategy, structure, emotion, or craft.
- **1–4 — Needs development:** material strategic or execution flaws prevent the copy from meeting commercial standards.
- Reserve **9** for unusually mature work with a distinctive competitive advantage; a **10** must be extremely rare.

## Business Power

Assess strategic precision and conversion potential:

- Define a specific audience and one measurable commercial objective.
- Address a real, deep, urgent problem without shifting audience or proposition.
- Focus on one concrete, credible benefit; support the USP with evidence.
- Make the CTA clear, low-friction, well-timed, and based on genuine urgency.
- Use credible proof, data, authority, and risk reversal rather than cheap trust signals.

## Reasoning Power

Assess persuasive structure and reading experience:

- Establish relevance quickly, foreground the core claim, and close decisively.
- Use a coherent persuasion path such as AIDA, PAS, or another suitable model.
- Give each paragraph one job; maintain smooth transitions, clear references, and a complete logic chain.
- Make the content scannable, grouped logically, and free of dense text walls.
- Bridge features to benefits, answer objections, and use representative evidence.

## Empathy Power

Assess emotional connection and brand warmth:

- Keep the emotional tone aligned with the brand and objective; build a coherent emotional arc.
- Center the audience through authentic situations, familiar language, and shared values.
- Use concrete, dynamic, sensory detail instead of abstract adjectives.
- Build narrative immersion with characters, conflict, turns, credible details, and conversational presence when appropriate.

## Innovation Power

Assess conceptual distinction and memorability:

- Avoid clichés and category jargon; create an ownable idea, framing, or name.
- Use a fresh entry point, contrast, reversal, or cross-category connection.
- Provide memorable lines, metaphors, labels, or repeatable slogans.
- Let format and interaction serve the idea; avoid obvious template writing.

## Word Power

Assess precision, force, rhythm, and finish:

- Prefer exact verbs and concrete nouns; restrain empty modifiers and corporate jargon.
- Remove redundancy, favor active voice, and vary sentence length while preserving parallel structure.
- Match brand voice; use punctuation and sound to guide pace.
- Check ambiguity, grammar, register clashes, repetition, and any cheap or generic tone.

## Calibration

Place the work in a global anchor band first, then adjust each dimension using the evidence above. Do not mechanically convert checklist counts into percentages. Use professional judgment and make the five scores reflect the copy's real relative strengths and weaknesses.
