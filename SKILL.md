# Five-Power Copy Audit

Judge the work against one question: “Can this copy help the client win business in a competitive market?”

## Workflow

1. Obtain the copy. If the audience, channel, or business objective is missing, state reasonable assumptions; never invent product evidence.
2. Read [references/rubric.md](references/rubric.md) in full and assess all five dimensions.
3. Assign an integer score from 1–10 to each dimension. Apply the global anchors first, then calibrate with evidence from the copy.
4. Identify the strongest and weakest dimensions. If scores tie, choose the dimension with the greatest likely commercial impact.
5. Use the output format below. Keep the Creative Director's Note under 120 words: lead with the strongest professional quality, identify the largest gap, and finish with one concrete revision.
6. After five samples in one conversation, calculate one-decimal averages and provide the writer-profile diagnosis. Treat the next sample as a new round.

## Single-copy output

```text
Five-Power Copy Audit
Business Power: X / 10
Reasoning Power: X / 10
Empathy Power: X / 10
Innovation Power: X / 10
Word Power: X / 10

Creative Director's Note
[Strongest quality, biggest gap, and one actionable revision]
```

## Five-sample diagnosis

Report one-decimal averages and select the best-supported profile:

- Writer: Empathy and Innovation clearly exceed Business.
- Seller: Business clearly exceeds Empathy and Word Power.
- Scholar: Reasoning clearly exceeds Word and Empathy.
- Veteran: most scores remain between 5–7 while Innovation trails.
- Beginner: most scores remain below 5 with no distinct strength.

If rules overlap, choose the profile supported by the largest score gap and the critique evidence. If none fits clearly, report a Hybrid profile instead of forcing a label.

## Quality controls

- Score only the supplied copy and context; do not treat missing context as a copy defect.
- Down-weight criteria that do not fit the format. A brand story may not require a hard CTA; a headline cannot carry a full narrative arc.
- Do not reward length, decorative language, or information volume by default.
- Ground ratings in observable evidence and avoid generic praise.
- Do not expose private chain-of-thought; provide concise findings and actionable recommendations.
